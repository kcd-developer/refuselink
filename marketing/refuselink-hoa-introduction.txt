Meet RefuseLink

A simpler way for HOA residents and community leaders to stay connected with KC Disposal.

RefuseLink gives you one convenient place to:

• See your next scheduled collection day
• View trash, recycling, and yard-waste service details
• Receive announcements from KC Disposal and your HOA
• Access helpful documents and service information
• Report missed pickups, cart issues, recycling problems, yard-waste concerns, service delays, and more
• Send private billing or account questions directly to KC Disposal
• Track request status, read responses, and continue the conversation
• View published HOA board and community-management contacts
• Manage multiple eligible service addresses from one account
• Use RefuseLink on a computer or add it to your phone like an app

Board members and community managers also receive secure tools to:

• View community service activity while protecting private resident conversations
• Publish announcements specifically for their community
• Keep residents informed about reminders, events, and service updates
• Review resident requests and escalate appropriate issues to KC Disposal
• Submit priority service-suspension and restoration requests
• Track requests through completion and identify suspended addresses
• Control which leadership contact details residents can see
• Switch between resident and leadership views when applicable

RefuseLink works in your browser and can be added to your phone for an app-like experience—no app-store download required.
